# How to play Launch Lab

Launch Lab is a free, fan-made **singleplayer map for Halo: Combat Evolved** in *Halo: The Master Chief Collection*
(MCC) on PC: a 3 km world of floating islands to climb by launching Warthogs with chain-reacting grenade piles, up to a
castle in the sky. It runs on the original game's engine and physics, so **you need your own copy of the game**.

## 1. What you need
- **A Windows PC with Steam.** The Microsoft Store / Xbox app version of MCC doesn't support mods.
- **Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary on Steam.** You don't need the whole collection: Halo CE Anniversary is sold on
  its own and installs inside *Halo: The Master Chief Collection*.
  [Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1064221)

## 2. Install the game parts it needs
1. Buy and install **Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary** (Steam installs *Halo: The Master Chief Collection* with it).
2. In your Steam Library, right-click **Halo: The Master Chief Collection** → **Properties** → **DLC** (or
   **Installed Content**) and make sure **both** are ticked:
   - **Halo: CE Campaign**
   - **Halo: CE Multiplayer**

   Launch Lab needs both; without the Multiplayer part, custom maps can load forever.
3. Start MCC once normally, so it finishes its first-time setup, then quit.

## 3. Get Launch Lab
1. Open the **Launch Lab page on the Steam Workshop**: *(link added when it's published)*.
2. Click **Subscribe**. Steam downloads it in the background. (Start MCC *after* the download finishes: MCC only
   notices new Workshop items when it starts.)

## 4. Play
1. In Steam, press **Play** on Halo: The Master Chief Collection and choose
   **"Anti-Cheat Disabled (Mods and Limited Services)"**. Mods are never loaded with anti-cheat on.
2. In MCC, open the **Campaigns** menu, choose **Halo: Combat Evolved**, and pick **Launch Lab**.

## How to play
- You start on the ground with two guns:
  - **The pile gun** (looks like a pistol): each shot drops a real frag grenade where it lands, without exploding.
    Build a pile, then throw one grenade at it: the whole pile goes off.
  - **The shield gun** (plasma pistol): drops overshields.
  - Ammo and grenades are **unlimited**.
- **Pile grenades under a Warthog, set them off, and ride the launch** up to the floating islands. Each level is higher,
  and the gaps get harder.
- **Checkpoints:** reaching a new level saves automatically. Switch your **flashlight on** to save wherever you are.
  If you die, you're back at your last checkpoint.
- Falling won't kill you in mid-air. Hitting the ground from high up will.
- The top level has a castle.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Launch Lab isn't in the menu | Launch with **Anti-Cheat Disabled**; make sure the Workshop download finished *before* starting MCC |
| Endless loading screen | Install **Halo: CE Multiplayer** as well (step 2) |
| "Mod does not support this version of the product" | MCC was updated: wait for an updated Launch Lab on the Workshop |
| Lag when setting off a very big grenade pile | That's the stock explosions: use smaller piles (a few dozen grenades is plenty) |
